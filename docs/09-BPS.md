# 9. BPS and local truth anchors

[README](../README.md) | Prev: [8. AI agents and structured instructions](08-AI-AGENTS.md) | Next: [10. Hosted layer and publishing](10-HOSTED-LAYER.md)

---

BPS means Beacon Positioning System / local truth anchors.

GPS is often not enough:

- indoors
- between tall buildings
- parking decks
- courtyards
- campuses
- warehouses
- hospitals
- event venues
- underground or covered areas

A Hashpath can point to local anchors where ordinary coordinates become unreliable.

Examples:

- “blue door”
- “loading dock 3”
- “north elevator”
- “orange cone”
- “Room 214”
- “scanner station”
- “Beacon B12”

BPS-style anchors can let a Hashpath continue after GPS stops being trustworthy.
