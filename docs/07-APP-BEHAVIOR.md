# 7. App behavior

[README](../README.md) | Prev: [6. Examples](06-EXAMPLES.md) | Next: [8. AI agents and structured instructions](08-AI-AGENTS.md)

---

The Hashpath app should make a procedure readable, shareable, and actionable.

Useful app behavior:

- show steps in order
- distinguish spatial points from non-spatial instructions
- open mappable steps in a map
- show access codes only where needed
- support sender preview
- support recipient reader mode
- copy/share the whole Hashpath
- allow step-by-step arrival
- avoid confusing current location with shared waypoints

## Sender view

The sender builds or edits the arrival procedure.

## Recipient view

The recipient receives a compact arrival card.

It should answer:

```text
Where do I start?
What do I do next?
Where do I finish?
What code or access detail do I need?
What should I avoid?
```
