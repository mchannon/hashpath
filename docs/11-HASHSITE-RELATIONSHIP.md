# 11. Relationship to Hashsite

[README](../README.md) | Prev: [10. Hosted layer and publishing](10-HOSTED-LAYER.md) | Next: [12. Roadmap and design principles](12-ROADMAP-DESIGN.md)

---

Hashsite and Hashpath are companion apps/products.

Bad framing:

```text
Hashsite is the app.
Hashpath is the grammar.
```

Better framing:

| Product | Primary job |
|---|---|
| Hashsite | Compact place codes |
| Hashpath | Compact arrival procedures |

Hashsite is for points.

Hashpath is for procedures around points.

Hashsite can give a gate, parking spot, stairwell, door, or pickup point a compact spatial handle.

Hashpath can arrange those handles and access details into a usable real-world arrival sequence.

## Simple distinction

```text
Hashsite: where is this?
Hashpath: how do I actually get there?
```

Both can have apps. Both can have docs. Both can have user-facing product surfaces.
