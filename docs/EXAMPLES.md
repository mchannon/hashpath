# Hashpath Examples

These examples are illustrative. The compact grammar is still draft.

## Single red waypoint

```text
#7B0055
```

Reader: destination #7B0055; open in maps; copy Hashsite; forward this location.

## Delivery with wrong driveway

```text
#7B0055jFERR>rDWgZAQc4729#p93Ld88K
```

Reader:

1. Do not use the wrong/deceptive driveway at #7BFERR.
2. Continue one-way over a dirt/washboard segment.
3. Use the correct gate at #7BZAQ.
4. Gate code: 4729#.
5. Park at #7B93L.
6. Leave food at the door/dropoff point #7B88K.

## Airbnb hidden key

```text
#7B0000pPARKkKEYvPlanterdDOORxEXIT
```

1. Park at #7BPARK.
2. Walk to the hidden key at #7BKEY.
3. Recognition cue: planter.
4. Use the key at front door #7BDOOR.
5. Exit through #7BEXIT.

## No-go rectangle

```text
#7B0055nFERRaZAQ
```

Reader: destination/base #7B0055; avoid the rectangular area from #7BFERR to #7BZAQ.

#7B0055mPICKvBrownFordExplorer

Reader: pickup point #7BPICK; look for brown Ford Explorer.

## Store picking with tare point

```text
#STOREtA7NqSHELF
```

Reader:

1. Go to Aisle 7 North tare point.
2. Say “here” or scan marker.
3. Walk forward to target shelf micro-region.
4. Pick the requested item.
5. Confirm by barcode/photo/voice.
